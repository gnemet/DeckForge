The company is majority held by its founder also acting as CEO. The Target considers itself the leading D2C mobile phone case brand in the CEE region. Its main markets are Romania, Hungary, Bulgaria, Greece, Slovakia, Poland. In 2024 the Target reported net sales of 13.2 million EUR and adjusted EBITDA of 3.3 million EUR, with a workforce of 61 employees. BDO is pleased to assist with financial and tax due diligence tasks and provide transaction advisory support upon request.
